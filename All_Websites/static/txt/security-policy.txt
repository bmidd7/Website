Security Policy

Reporting
---------
Security vulnerabilities should be reported to:
mailto:B.rad.M.Website@gmail.com

Please include:
- A description of the vulnerability
- The affected URL or component
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Potential impact
- Any relevant screenshots, logs, or proof of concept

Scope
-----
This policy applies to:
- bmiddleton.dev
- *.bmiddleton.dev

Out of Scope
------------
The following are not considered security vulnerabilities:
- Social engineering
- Spam
- Physical attacks
- Issues affecting third-party services

Response
--------
Reports will be reviewed as soon as reasonably possible.
Additional information may be requested when necessary.

Disclosure
----------
Please allow reasonable time for investigation and remediation
before publicly disclosing a vulnerability.

Acknowledgment
--------------
Researchers who responsibly report valid vulnerabilities may be
credited with their permission. They will be found at:
https://bmiddleton.dev/acknowledgments
